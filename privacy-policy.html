Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-18

This Privacy Policy describes how the Video Upload API (“the application”) handles information when connecting to Google Drive.

Information the application accesses

With your authorization, the application may access Google account and Google Drive information required for its features. This may include OAuth credentials used to maintain authorization, file metadata, and video files selected for upload. The exact access depends on the permissions shown on Google's consent screen.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate with Google Drive, upload video files, inspect files or folders needed by the application, and provide file links or sharing settings requested by the user.

Storage and sharing

Uploaded videos are stored in the Google Drive account and destination folder configured by the user. If the user asks the application to enable “anyone with the link” access, people who obtain that link may be able to view the file. The user is responsible for choosing files and sharing settings.

OAuth tokens may be stored on the server to maintain authorization. The operator should restrict access to those tokens and protect them as credentials. Token files must not be published or shared.

Disclosure and sale

The application operator does not sell personal information or disclose it to third parties except as needed to provide the requested Google Drive functionality, comply with law, or protect the application and its users.

Retention and deletion

Files uploaded through the application remain in Google Drive until the user deletes them or changes their storage settings. OAuth access can be revoked in the user's Google Account security settings. Revoking access may stop the application from working until authorization is granted again.

Security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect server access, OAuth credentials, and uploaded content. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children

This application is not intended to be directed at children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.
